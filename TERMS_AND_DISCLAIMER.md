## 2. Consult a Licensed Nigerian Legal Practitioner
Legal problems depend heavily on unique factual situations, procedural rules, court rules, judicial precedents, and specific jurisdictional issues across Nigerian Federal and State High Courts, the National Industrial Court, the Court of Appeal, and the Supreme Court of Nigeria.

If you are dealing with:
* An active police investigation, arrest, or criminal allegation;
* Commercial litigation, contracts, or debt recovery;
* Employment termination or industrial trade disputes;
* Property, land registration, or corporate restructuring;

**You must seek formal counsel from a qualified Legal Practitioner enrolled at the Supreme Court of Nigeria.**
